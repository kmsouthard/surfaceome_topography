Sources and attribution
Heights
UniProt sequences and topology, AlphaFold DB models (Jumper et al. 2021; Varadi et al. 2024), Pfam domains, MobiDB disorder, and glycosites from UniProt, GlyGen and GlycoMine.
Protein annotations
Location and transmembrane count: UniProt. Surface call and CD numbers: SURFY (Bausch-Fluck et al. 2018, PMID 30373828). Functional class: Almén et al. 2009 (PMID 19678920).
Protein drawings
CellScape (Silvestre-Ryan, Fletcher and Holmes 2022, bioRxiv).
Cell surfaces
Expression Atlas E-PROT-1 (Kim et al. 2014, PMID 24870542) and E-PROT-27 (breast tumours, Tyanova et al. 2016, PMID 26725330). Monocyte subsets: Ravenhill et al. 2020 (PMID 32165698).
Interactions
STRING and CellphoneDB pairs, annotated here as cis or trans. Interaction class: CellphoneDB.
Antibody epitopes
PDB structures, or the paper cited beside each antibody.
Rules
Exclusion at 5 nm taller than the gap: Schmid et al. 2016 (PMID 27980602). 10 nm phagocytosis range: Bakalar et al. 2018 (PMID 29958103). Kinetic segregation: Davis and van der Merwe 2006 (PMID 16855606); Cordoba et al. 2013 (PMID 23580664).
